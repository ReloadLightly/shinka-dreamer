<DESCRIPTION>
Add a sequential change detector using the anonymous transition likelihoods already computed by the mixture. Accumulate evidence when an equal mixture of the uniform and fast experts predicts observations better than the slow expert, normalized by informative enemy count. Require sustained support and a cooldown before rebuilding the fast learner from four recent informative transitions.

Recovery preserves the slow learner, terrain-change exclusions, probability floors, and learning intervention. The refreshed dynamics feed both existing planning and exported predictions. This targets delayed adaptation without discarding useful stationary-law evidence; improvement remains an empirical hypothesis.
</DESCRIPTION>
